Donggeun Kwon is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Information Engineering at Kunsan National University, where he leads the Cybersecurity Lab. He received his Ph.D. from the Graduate School of Information Security at Korea University, and has published in leading international and domestic venues including ACNS. He is actively recruiting graduate and undergraduate research students interested in security.

Research Interests

  • Cryptography & Side-Channel Analysis: Design and security analysis of symmetric-key, public-key, post-quantum, and lightweight ciphers, together with attacks and countermeasures that exploit power and electromagnetic side-channel leakage from cryptographic devices.
  • AI-based Security: Leveraging artificial intelligence and deep learning techniques for a broad range of information security applications, including side-channel analysis, cryptanalysis, and threat detection.
  • Key Topics: Differential cryptanalysis, deep-learning-based side-channel analysis, and efficient and secure cryptographic implementations on ARM, AVR, and FPGA platforms.
