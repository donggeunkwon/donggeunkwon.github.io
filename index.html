I am an Assistant Professor at Kunsan National University, where I lead the Crypto and Computer Security Lab. My research focuses on cryptography, side-channel analysis, and AI-based security. I received my Ph.D. in Cyber Security from Korea University in 2025.

Research interests: Cryptanalysis · Side-channel attacks and countermeasures · Post-quantum cryptography · AI-based security · Secure implementations
